About Us

Local roots. Lasting results.

A family-owned team that treats your property with the same care and attention we give our own.

  • Local Tri-State Area Expertise

    Based in South Point, Ohio, our fully insured father-and-son team, Tim and Tyler Porter, proudly serves Southern Ohio, Eastern Kentucky, and Western West Virginia. We know the area, and we know what it takes to keep local properties looking their best.

  • Diverse Skill Set for Every Need

    From house and roof washing to driveways, gutters, and general handyman work, we help homeowners and businesses keep their properties in shape. We bring the right equipment, practical experience, and attention to detail to every job.

  • Family-Driven Commitment to Quality

    Family-owned since 2022, we believe good work starts with treating people right. Our customers are our neighbors, and we take pride in earning their trust. Our guiding principle comes from Colossians 3:23: “And whatsoever ye do, do it heartily, as to the Lord, and not unto men.”
