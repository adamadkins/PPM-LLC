Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Our commitment to making information about property maintenance easier to access.

Our approach

Porter Property Maintenance, LLC aims to make its website usable for as many visitors as possible, including people who use keyboards, screen readers, or assistive technologies.

Accessibility features

The website is designed with responsive layouts, labeled form fields, keyboard-operable navigation, visible focus indicators, and reduced-motion support. Accessibility can vary by device, browser, and third-party service.

Known limitations

Some embedded or third-party services, including the photo uploader and external form processing, may not provide the same accessibility experience as the rest of the website. We have not completed a formal independent accessibility audit and do not claim certification or full conformance with a particular standard.

Need assistance?

If you encounter a barrier, need information in another format, or have trouble submitting a request, please describe the issue through our contact page. Include the page and what you were trying to do so the issue can be investigated.

Ongoing improvements

We intend to review accessibility as the website changes and improve issues that are identified.
