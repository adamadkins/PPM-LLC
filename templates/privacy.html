Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

How information submitted through this website may be collected, used, and shared.

Information you provide

When you use our contact or estimate forms, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, property address, property type, requested services, project details, and optional photos. Please do not submit sensitive personal or financial information through these forms.

How information is used

Information submitted through the website is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate service requests, prepare estimates, communicate about potential work, and help identify unwanted or abusive submissions.

Service providers

Our website uses Formspark to process contact and estimate form submissions and Uploadcare to handle optional photo uploads. Information you submit may be processed by those services according to their respective policies. Their policies are available at Formspark and Uploadcare.

Security and retention

We take reasonable care when handling inquiries, but no website or online submission method can guarantee complete security. Submitted information may be retained as needed to respond to requests, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations. Specific retention periods should be confirmed with the business owner and its service providers.

Cookies and third-party services

Third-party form and upload services may use technical information or cookies needed to operate their services. This website also links to Facebook; following that link takes you to a separate website governed by its own policies. Any additional analytics, advertising, or tracking tools should be disclosed here if enabled in the future.

Your privacy questions

To ask about information you have submitted, request a correction, or inquire about deletion, please use our contact page. Requests will be handled subject to applicable law and legitimate business recordkeeping needs.

Changes

We may update this policy as the website and our practices change. The date shown above identifies this version.
